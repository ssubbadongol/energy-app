5. Moderation, reporting and blocking

Every pod message is automatically screened before it settles. On top of that:

  • Report anything. Press and hold a message to report it. Reports go to a human.
  • Block anyone. Blocking hides everything they post from you, immediately, and keeps you out of rooms with them in future.
  • We act within 24 hours. We review reports of objectionable content within 24 hours and remove anything that breaks these rules.
  • We remove people. Accounts that post objectionable content or abuse other members are ejected, without refund and without warning where the breach is serious.

You can also reach us directly at support.softfocus@gmail.com.
