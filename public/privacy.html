Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Soft Focus is an app for managing tasks and energy on days when that is hard. Some of what you put into it is personal, and some of it is about your mental health. This page explains exactly what we hold, why, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it.

It is written to be read, not to be survived. If anything here is unclear, email support.softfocus@gmail.com and ask.

Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Shashank Subba Dongol, contactable at support.softfocus@gmail.com. Soft Focus is operated from the United Kingdom and this policy is written to meet the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

You do not need an account

When you first open Soft Focus it creates an anonymous account — a random identifier with no name, no email address and nothing linking it to you. Tasks, reminders, the timer and your daily list all work this way. We never ask who you are.

You only create a real account if you subscribe to Soft Focus Pro. At that point we need an email address, because a subscription that cannot follow you to a new phone is a subscription you would lose. That is the only reason we ask for it.

The Soft Focus mentor

The mentor is powered by Google's Gemini model. When you send it a message, that message — along with your recent conversation, your first name, your tags and goals, and your current task list — is sent to the Gemini API so it can reply.

This means that whatever you tell the mentor is transmitted to Google. People use this mentor to talk about being overwhelmed, stuck, low, or worse, so we want that stated plainly rather than buried.

Soft Focus currently uses the free Gemini API tier, and you should know what that means before you type anything into the mentor.

On the free tier, Google may use your prompts and the model's replies to improve its own products and models, and human reviewers at Google may read them. That is Google's policy for free API usage, not a choice we make message by message — but it is our choice to be on that tier, so we are telling you plainly rather than letting you assume otherwise.

So: please don't put anything into the mentor that you would not want a stranger at Google to read. If you want to talk about something you would rather nobody saw, talk to a person instead — the support page lists free, confidential UK helplines.

We intend to move to the paid tier, where Google excludes prompts and responses from training and human review. When we do, this section will change and the date at the top of this page will change with it. Until you see that, assume the above.

Separately from Google: we do not use your conversations to train anything, we never sell them, and nobody at Soft Focus reads them — with the single exception of the pod moderation case described below, which does not cover mentor conversations at all.

Pods, and how they are moderated

Pods are small, temporary, anonymous rooms. You appear under an alias the app assigns; other members do not learn your identity, and you do not learn theirs.

Every message is automatically checked for safety before it settles. Two things can follow:

  • Distress. If a message suggests you may be in crisis, the app quietly offers you support resources. The message is not deleted and no one else is told.
  • Abuse. If a message is abusive, it is hidden from the room and kept in a private log for review.

Members can also report a message. Reported and flagged messages — including their original text, the alias, and the account identifiers of the author and the reporter — are retained so we can act on them, and so that a pattern of abuse across several rooms is visible. We keep these even if the account is later deleted; that is a legitimate interest in keeping the rooms safe and in defending against abuse, and it is the one exception to deletion below.

Everything else in a pod is deleted when the pod closes — 24 hours or 7 days after it opens, depending on the room.

Deleting your account and your data

You can delete everything from inside the app: Settings → Delete account. It is immediate and it is not reversible. Your profile, tasks, mentor history and pod membership are erased, and your login is destroyed.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Only Apple or Google can do that, because only they hold the billing relationship. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings first, or you will keep being charged for an app you no longer have an account for.

If you no longer have the app installed, use the account deletion page.

Two things survive deletion, for the reasons given above: moderation records for any message that was reported or flagged, and anonymised, aggregate figures that cannot be traced back to you.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Ask what we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Have your data erased — see above.
  • Restrict or object to how we use it, including objecting to anything we do on the basis of legitimate interests.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis.

Email support.softfocus@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

Soft Focus is not for under-16s. It includes anonymous conversation with strangers and discussion of mental health, and neither is something we are equipped to offer to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16; if you believe a child is using the app, email us and we will delete the account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Requests from the app are attested with Firebase App Check, so a modified client or a script cannot reach your data even with a stolen token. Access to the production database is limited to the people who operate the service.

No system is perfect. If a breach puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the ICO within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
