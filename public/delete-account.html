Delete your Soft Focus account

Last updated 15 September 2026

You can delete your account and everything in it. There are two ways, and the first one is instant.

Cancel your subscription first. If you subscribe to Soft Focus Pro, deleting your account does not stop the billing — only Apple or Google can do that, because only they hold your payment details.

Cancel in App Store subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions, then come back and delete the account.

If you still have the app

Open Soft Focus and go to:

Settings → Delete account

You'll be asked to confirm. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

If you've already uninstalled it

Email support.softfocus@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up, with the subject Account deletion request.

We'll confirm within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days. We ask you to email from your registered address because it is the only way we can tell it's really your account — we hold no other identifying information about you.

If you only ever used Soft Focus without an account, there is nothing to request: your data was tied to an anonymous identifier stored on your device, and uninstalling the app orphaned it. Email us anyway if you'd like it purged and we will do it.

What gets deleted

  • Your login and email address
  • Your profile — name, energy tags, goals, mentor tone
  • All of your tasks and reminders
  • Your entire mentor conversation history
  • Your pod memberships
  • Your subscription record on our side

What is kept, and why

Two things survive, and we would rather say so than pretend otherwise:

  • Moderation records. If one of your pod messages was reported or flagged for abuse, that record is retained for up to 12 months. Pods are anonymous rooms, and letting deletion erase the evidence would make deletion a tool for abusers.
  • Anonymised aggregates. Counts and totals that cannot be traced back to any individual.

Everything else is gone. Backups are overwritten on a 7-day cycle, so deleted data clears those within a week.

Full detail is in the Privacy Policy. Questions: support.softfocus@gmail.com.
