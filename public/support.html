If you need to talk to someone right now

Soft Focus is an app, and this page is not a crisis service. These are:

  • Samaritans — 116 123, free, 24/7, any kind of distress. samaritans.org
  • Shout — text SHOUT to 85258, if talking is too much. giveusashout.org
  • NHS 111 — urgent mental health help, choose option 2.
  • 999 — if life is in immediate danger.
  • Outside the UK: findahelpline.com

Contact us

support.softfocus@gmail.com

One person reads this inbox, so it is not instant — but it is a real person and you will get a reply. Expect a response within 2 working days.

Reporting something in a pod

If someone is being abusive or posting something that shouldn't be there, the fastest route is inside the app: press and hold the message → Report. That reaches us straight away and we review reports within 24 hours.

You can also block anyone from the same menu. Blocking hides everything they post from you immediately and keeps you out of future rooms with them.

If you would rather email, write to support.softfocus@gmail.com with the subject Pod report and tell us roughly when it happened and what the room was about.

Common things

I paid for Pro and it hasn't unlocked

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. If that doesn't do it within a minute, email us with the store you bought from and roughly when.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Through the store, not through us: App Store or Google Play. We can't cancel it on your behalf, and neither can we issue refunds — those are Apple's and Google's to give.

I got a new phone and everything's gone

If you had an account, sign in and it comes back. If you were using Soft Focus without one, the data was tied to your old device and unfortunately it does not transfer — that is exactly why Pro requires an account.

How do I delete my account?

Here. Cancel your subscription with the store first.

The mentor says it's paused

The AI features run on a fixed monthly budget and pause automatically when it's reached, rather than running up a bill. Tasks, the timer and everything else keep working. It resets at the start of the month.
